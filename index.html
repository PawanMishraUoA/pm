01 · ABOUT

Academic profile

Dr. Pawan Mishra is an academician and researcher in Computer Science, currently serving as an Assistant Professor in the School of Computer Science and Applications at Sharda University, Greater Noida, India. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Allahabad, where his research focused on Artificial Intelligence, Soft Computing Algorithms, Robotic Path Planning and Multi-Objective Optimization.

His academic career includes teaching and research experience at Galgotias University, Greater Noida Institute of Technology, and JSS Academy of Technical Education, Noida. Prior to his academic career, he worked as a Program Analyst/Data Scientist at WNS Global Services, Noida, contributing to projects involving Python, data analysis, recommendation systems and software applications.

His current research focuses on intelligent optimization techniques and hybrid computational models for complex real-world problems, with particular interest in robotic path planning, autonomous navigation, dynamic environments, healthcare analytics and energy optimization.
